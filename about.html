Who We Are

EarthWise is an educational project focused on teaching environmental sustainability with the aim of people understand and adopt sustainable practices in everyday life.

Our Story

EarthWise began in 2025 as a community project to raise awareness about climate change and sustainable living. We noticed that many people wanted to help the planet, but didn't know where to start. That's why we created free lessons and quizzes as well as resources to guide individuals step by step.

Our Values

  • Promoting eco-friendly living
  • Making sustainability education accessible
  • Encouraging action for a greener future

Our Goals

  • Increase awareness about recycling and waste reduction.
  • Promote the use of renewable energy worldwide.
  • Encourage eco-friendly habits in households and schools.
  • Support global initiatives like the UN Sustainable Development Goals.
